# Terms Of Service

**Product characterisation.** MegaPush does not pay out cash from gameplay under any circumstance. Staked funds return exclusively as lottery entries purchased on the user's behalf from a third-party protocol; only deposited-but-unstaked funds are withdrawable as USDC. Whether this constitutes gambling, and under which framework, is the central question.

**Relationship to Megapot.** MegaPush is an independent third party. Megapot's application is a licensed operator; MegaPush is not, and must not imply otherwise. Clear disclaimers of endorsement are required.

**Custody.** MegaPush custodies unstaked play balances in escrow only. It does not custody tickets or winnings. The escrow permits unilateral user withdrawal.

**Jurisdictional restrictions.** Which markets are served, which are blocked, and how blocking is enforced.

**KYC and age verification.** MegaPush mirrors Megapot's $3,000 threshold on cumulative deposits. Counsel to confirm this is sufficient for the target markets, and to advise on age verification, which is currently unaddressed.

**Testnet disclaimer.** During testnet, tickets carry no value and no real drawing entry.

**Limitation of liability** for smart contract bugs, chain reorganisation, oracle or RPC failure, and third-party protocol changes.

**Dispute resolution** and governing law.

**Account closure and self-exclusion**, including the irreversibility of self-exclusion and the guarantee that withdrawal and claiming remain available after exclusion.
