# Responsible Gaming

MegaPush should be entertainment. These tools are here so you can keep it that way. All of them are in Settings and none require contacting support.

## Tools available now

**Deposit limit.** Set your own daily cap below the default `[MAXDEP]`. Lowering it takes effect immediately. Raising it takes 24 hours.

**Loss limit.** Set a maximum you're willing to lose in a day. When you hit it, staking is disabled until the next day.

**Session reminder.** A prompt after a chosen interval showing time played and net result.

**Cooling-off.** Lock yourself out for 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days. Cannot be reversed once started. Withdrawals still work throughout.

**Self-exclusion.** Permanent lockout of your wallet and account. Cannot be reversed by us or by you. Your remaining balance and any tickets you hold are unaffected. You can still withdraw and still claim.

All of these are in Settings. None require contacting support.

## When to use them

* Staking more than you planned, or more than you can comfortably lose
* Playing to win back losses
* Playing to change how you feel rather than for entertainment
* Hiding how much you play from people close to you
* Borrowing to play, or playing with money meant for something else
* Feeling like you cannot stop when you decide to

None of these are moral failings. They are common, they are well understood, and there is effective help for them.

## Getting help

Support for gambling problems is free and confidential in most countries. Local services vary, so we would rather point you to a current directory than list numbers that may be out of date.

The **Gambling Therapy** online service operates internationally in multiple languages: [gamblingtherapy.org](https://www.gamblingtherapy.org/)

{% hint style="warning" %}
If you are in immediate distress, contact local emergency services or a crisis line in your country.
{% endhint %}

## If someone you know is affected

Support services almost always help family members and friends as well as players, and you do not need the person's permission to reach out.
