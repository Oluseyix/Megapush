# Why MegaPush Exists

Megapot runs one drawing a day. You buy a ticket, and then you wait twenty-four hours. It is a good product with a thin loop. Nothing happens between the purchase and the draw.

**MegaPush is what happens in between.**

## Two problems we're trying to solve

**Buying a lottery ticket is boring.** The purchase is the least interesting part of playing the lottery. The interesting part is the anticipation, and that all sits in the drawing. MegaPush moves some of that tension forward: before you're waiting on the draw, you're already sweating a multiplier.

**Crash games have nothing behind them.** They're played by enormous numbers of people, and they're a closed loop: money in, money out, nothing left over. When a MegaPush round goes your way, you don't get cash. You get an entry in a real drawing with a real prize pool that pays out to real people every day. The upside continues past the game.

## The bet we're making

That people who already play crash games and people who already play lotteries are largely the same people, and that combining the two is more interesting than either alone.

## What makes MegaPush different

Four things, and we intend to keep all four:

* **Winnings come as tickets.** No cash exit from gameplay, ever. MegaPush is a way to get more lottery entries, not a casino.
* **97% RTP, and we earn more when you win.** Our referral revenue outweighs what we keep from busted rounds.
* **Every round is verifiable.** Committed before betting, revealed after the crash, checkable by anyone.
* **Your funds and tickets are yours.** Non-custodial on both ends.
